Quick help

How do I start riding?

Choose Practice a line to start standing and learn to steer by drawing across the wave face. For the catch sequence, choose Catch a wave, pull down and keep holding through the paddle strokes, then release at the green stand prompt.

If that prompt never appears, report the build and what you tried. You can use Practice a line to continue testing the ride.

I cannot hear the game.

Check the game's sound and music options, the phone's media volume, Silent Mode and its current audio output. Bluetooth headphones can remain connected even when you expect the speaker.

If sound is still missing, tell us whether it failed from launch or after an interruption, and whether the speaker and headphones behave differently.

Can I make the controls more forgiving?

Open Options and move the handling setting toward Arcade. Move toward Simulation for a more demanding ride. Tell us which setting you used when reporting a trick that feels unreliable.

Where are my progress and photographs?

This beta keeps its progress and saved photographs on your device. Revisit saved covers in the cover archive. Share or export pictures you want to keep elsewhere.

Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step unless you are prepared to lose its local game data. This beta does not promise cloud-save recovery.

How do I report a crash or send a screenshot?

If you installed through TestFlight, use its feedback flow. You can also email hello@kingmade.co. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Highline version/build, the mode you were playing and what happened just before the problem.

Check screenshots and videos for personal information before attaching them. A short clip of the failure is especially useful for missed touches, stuttering and unusual landings.

What does this beta cost?

The first native beta is planned with all current content accessible and no ads or purchases. Future release pricing and content will be described separately when decided.

A useful report takes one minute.

  1. Describe what you expected and what actually happened.
  2. Give us the shortest steps that reproduce it.
  3. Include the device, iOS version and game build.
  4. Tell us if it happened once, sometimes or every time.

For a privacy question or a request about information you have emailed to us, contact the same address with the subject “Highline privacy.”
