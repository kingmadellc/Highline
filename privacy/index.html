Privacy in Highline.

Highline is made by King Made. The unrestricted native beta is designed to work without an account, advertising or purchases.

Effective 5 October 2026. Applies to the Highline TestFlight beta and will be updated before any purchase or online service is added.

Information stored on your device

Highline stores settings, progress, ride history, replay records, local playtest events and selected photographs in its local app storage. Highline does not automatically upload gameplay records to King Made, use advertising identifiers, or include advertising, analytics or purchase services.

Sharing and links

If you choose to export or share a photograph, wave invitation or playtest record, Highline provides that selected item to the destination you choose. The destination app or service controls how it handles that information. Highline does not automatically publish your photographs or contacts.

Opening a source or license link takes you to an external website in your browser. That website's privacy practices apply. Device backups can contain local app data according to your Apple settings.

Your choices

Ride journal lets you clear ride history and local playtest events. Cover archive lets you delete archive photographs. Photographs pinned in the summer issue can remain associated with that issue.

Removing Highline removes its local app data from the device. Copies you exported or kept in a device backup are controlled separately. This beta does not offer cloud-save recovery.

Beta feedback and support

If you send TestFlight feedback or a crash report, Apple processes it through TestFlight and makes the feedback available to the developer. Apple also provides platform diagnostics according to your settings and its terms.

If you email support, King Made receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate your report. Please avoid including personal information in screenshots or exported playtest files.

Contact hello@kingmade.co with questions or requests about support information you have shared.

Changes

If later versions add purchases or other services, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated to describe the actual behavior.
