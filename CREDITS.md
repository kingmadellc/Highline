# Credits

Highline is a King Made surfing prototype.

Movement sounds adapt **Ocean splash** by **Thimras**, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original asset and author](https://opengameart.org/content/ocean-splash). The source was trimmed, filtered, faded and mixed into short release, re-entry and wipeout effects.

Barlow and the renamed Highline Tide adaptation of Fraunces use SIL Open Font License fonts. Their notices are included in [the release](releases/0.16.0/StreamingAssets/Highline-font-licenses.txt), with the full [Barlow license](releases/0.16.0/Brand/Barlow-OFL.txt) and [Fraunces license](releases/0.16.0/Brand/Fraunces-OFL.txt).

From v0.20.0, crest stamp callouts use **Shrikhand** by Jonny Pinhorn, an SIL Open Font License font; its notice is included in [the release font notices](releases/0.20.0/StreamingAssets/Highline-font-licenses.txt).

From v0.22.0 the surfer's body is built from the **realistic male** base mesh in Blender Studio's [Human Base Meshes Bundle](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/#assets) (CC0 1.0), scaled, reshaped, textured and rigged by the studio's build script.
